Kotimaan Teknokatsaus XVI

2026 | VJ Performance

I was invited to participate in the 2026 edition of the Kotimaan Teknokatsaus XVI (Finnish domestic technology review), a recurring series reviewing VJ culture and practice in Finland. The proceeds from this event are always donated to charity.

I collaborated with Ana Gutieszca. She provided the music for the performance.
