# VJ for Tokyo Ry

2022 | VJ Performance

The association of art students at Aalto University, TOKYO RY, organizes a big party around the end of fall every year. I collaborated with them for both the stage design (visuals) and as a VJ, along other artists too. I am always looking for bigger and bigger venues, and this time I had the chance to use 5 large-format projectors to display my AI visuals, real-time reactive generative art and live video mixing.
