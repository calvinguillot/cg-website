# VJ for Rosa Jules

2022 | VJ Performance

[Rosa Jules](https://www.instagram.com/rosajulesgoes/), with her incredible versatility for composition and creation of catchy and impactful songs, is something to experience firsthand. I collaborated with her for this performance, and created real-time visuals that reacted to her beautiful melodies.
