# Dome Dreaming Festival / Takeoff

2022-2026 | 360° Single-channel Video and Performance

I was invited to participate in the 2026 edition of the [Dome Dreaming Film Festival](https://domedreaming.com/) in Stockholm. My proejct Takeoff was onece again presented. The programme brings together international artists working at the intersection of cinema, music, and immersive technology, exploring the unique possibilities of the dome as an artistic medium.

Dome Dreaming is a non-profit fulldome film festival produced by [Jonas Johansson](https://jonasjohansson.se/), Fredrik Edström and Sebastian Häger, in collaboration with Aavistus, Kokong, Baltic Analog Lab and Nordic Audiovisual Artists.
