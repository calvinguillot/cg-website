# Healing

2026 | Ink on canvas/ paper (pen plotter)

After an interesting end to 2025, I decided to go back to basics and made about twenty prints on my modified pen plotter (took 9 months but still). Some are animals, some are generative abstract art, and some are just portraits. Precious mementos for friends and family.
