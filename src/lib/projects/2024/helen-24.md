# Helen 24

2024 | Stencil Spray Paint on Metal

Helsinki’s electric company (Helen Oy) has ownership of several hundreds of electric cabinets across the city. As part of their corporate social initiative they encourage artists to paint these cabinets. I’ve been painting several boxes across the city since 2018. Below are most of them.

Most of them were made in collaboration with [Monika Hauck](https://www.instagram.com/monautomaton/), [Margo Nowicka](https://margonowicka.com/), Miguel García, and Kalle Eskelinen.
