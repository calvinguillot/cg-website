# Consumed — Art for All Festival

_2025 | Itäkeskus, Helsinki, Finland_
_Single-channel video, 2 × 1 m_

Video installation for the Art For All Festival 2025 at the Easton Hansakäytävä shopping centre, on the theme of _Success & Glory_ — exploring money, consumerism, and what success means in contemporary society. The piece presents a bird's-eye view of the unstoppable growth of shopping centres around Eastern Helsinki, with simulated geometry amalgamated with real aerial photography and geospatial map data. Asks: how much more will we build before it is enough?

Many problems in our modern world can be attributed to a single root: unrestricted consumption, encouraged and fundamentally necessary for the current form of capitalism. The shopping mall is a symbol of wasteful city planning, where new venues, metro stations, and residential areas catalyse the emergence of shopping malls even adjacent to existing ones.

_Technologies: 3D simulation, geospatial data visualisation, digital compositing, aerial photography integration._
