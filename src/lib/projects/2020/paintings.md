# Paintings

2017-2020 | Various techniques

For many reasons during 2020, I had some extra time at home. I created paintings for some of my close friends during that summer. Similarly, some paintings and stencils I did during 2019 and before. There is no much before 2016. I lost some pictures and files, and I never took pictures of many things that I did. But it could be that maybe they are in some hard drive in Colombia or somewhere else.

![Eagle](../../../assets/2020/eagle.webp)

Eagle: Spray paint stencil on canvas. 65cm x 45cm. Espoo, 2020. Albania flag is my favourite flag. I think people from that country, as my friend, are very strong, kind, and unique.

![Koira M](../../../assets/2020/koira-m.webp)

Koria M: Spray paint stencil on canvas. 65cm x 45cm. Espoo, 2020. Maya is a beautiful dog.

Koria T: Spray paint stencil on canvas. 65cm x 45cm. Espoo, 2020. Koira group will last forever.

![Vaca](../../../assets/2020/vaca.webp)

Vaca:  Spray paint stencil on canvas. 35cm x 20cm. Espoo, 2020. Sometimes, you just have to go away.

![Small Set](../../../assets/2020/small-paints.webp)

Small Set: Spray paint stencil on canvas. 35cm x 20cm. Espoo, 2020. I think everyone has a favourite animal. I always ask the people around me which one it is. We never know when can I gift them one.

![Her by Roy](../../../assets/2020/roy.webp)

Her by Roy: Spray paint stencil on canvas. 90cm x 65cm. Espoo, 2020. Roy Lichtenstein had a particular aesthetic that I really like. I feel that the girl with a hair ribbon is someone that I've been always looking for. I think I perhaps, I finally found her.

![Waiting](../../../assets/2020/waiting.webp)

Waiting: Spray paint stencil on plaster. 10cm x 30cm. Helsinki, 2018. Sometimes I think in only one thing. No matter how chaotic my surrounding is.

![Resilience](../../../assets/2020/elephant.webp)

Resilience: Spray paint stencil on canvas. 65cm x 90cm. Espoo, 2019. We all have the capacity to recover quickly from difficulties. We just have to keep on going.

![Thales](../../../assets/2020/thales.webp)

Thales: Spray paint stencil on canvas. 90cm x 65cm. Helsinki, 2018. As the mind get older, it transforms, decomposes, and reborns.

Autumn '17:  Spray paint stencil on canvas.  and 3D printing. Varied sizes. Helsinki, 2017.

The Creation. Spray paint stencil on metal. 134cm x 103cm. Helsinki, 2017. Man created God in his own image, in the image of Man he created God.
