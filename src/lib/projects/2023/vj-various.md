# VJ for Various

2023 | VJ Performance

As my artistic practice has diversified, I have gotten the opportunity to work with several musical artists, at different events and raves across the Helsinki area. Below you can find some pictures from when i worked in 2023 with [Lala Salama](https://www.instagram.com/lalasalama666/), [ERBR](https://www.instagram.com/dj_br1uno/), [Entropy RY](https://www.instagram.com/entropy_ry/), and some other improvised events.
