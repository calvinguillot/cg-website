# Fields — Perlin Noise Generative Art

_2021 | Online / p5.js_

Generative art series exploring Perlin noise fields in p5.js. Each field's colours are associated with states of feeling. A recurring aesthetic element reused across multiple later projects.

_Technologies: p5.js, Perlin noise algorithm._
_Interactive: [editor.p5js.org/calvinguillot/full/VIUTrnhkt](https://editor.p5js.org/calvinguillot/full/VIUTrnhkt)_
