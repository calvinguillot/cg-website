# Fields

2021 | p5js

A notable technique to use fro generative art is Perlin noise, an algorithm developed by Ken Perlin in the 1980s. Perlin noise generates natural-looking textures on computer-generated surfaces, making it immensely useful for many applications. My exploration focuses on associatin the fields and its colors with how iwas feeling at the moment. This style has been used and will be used I’m sure, in many more projects of mine.
