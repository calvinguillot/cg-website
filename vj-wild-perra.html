VJ for Wild Perra — Tokyo RY, Aalto University

2023 | VJ Performance

The association of art students at Aalto University, TOKYO RY, organizes a big party around the end of fall every year. I collaborated with Wild Perra for a set. They are a shapeshifting musician, who can blend horror, love, and absurdism in their music.
